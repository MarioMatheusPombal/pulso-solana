Amber means a human decision is needed. It never communicates “approved”. Green means confirmed; coral/red means denied or failed. Color pairs need icons or labels too.

Motion / 20-second storyboard

Request → exact payload → human decision → result.

0–3s agent requests; 3–8s exact fields appear; 8–13s human reviews; 13–16s one-time approval; 16–20s observed devnet result and notice. Use restrained 180–240ms transitions. Keep payload stable while reviewed. Honor reduced motion.
